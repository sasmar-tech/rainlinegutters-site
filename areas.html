Local conditions

Three counties, three sets of gutter problems.

The same storm hits a canopy street in Carrollwood, a barrier-side home in Dunedin, and a new build in Trinity very differently.

  • Hillsborough: mature live oaks and older fascia — guards and fascia repair come up often.
  • Pinellas: salt air and coastal wind — aluminum and hurricane-rated hangers matter most.
  • Pasco: large new-construction roofs and pine debris — capacity and downspout placement are key.

FAQ

Gutter questions, answered plainly.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between seamless and sectional gutters?

Sectional gutters come in pre-cut lengths — often 10 feet — joined with connectors and sealant. Every one of those joints is a place for water to drip, debris to catch, and the seal to fail in Florida heat.

Seamless gutters are rolled on site from a continuous aluminum coil, cut to the exact length of each run. The only joints are at corners and end caps, which we seal by hand.

Do I need 5" or 6" gutters in Florida?

It depends on your roof, not a rule of thumb. 5" K-style works well on many homes with moderate roof areas and simpler rooflines. Large roofs, steep pitches, long runs, and corners where valleys concentrate water often overflow a 5" gutter in a summer downpour.

A 6" gutter carries roughly 40% more water per foot and pairs with larger 3"×4" downspouts. We measure first, then recommend — sometimes 6" only on the runs that need it. Compare sizes.

Are gutter guards worth it?

If you have oaks, pines, or palms near the roof, usually yes. Guards keep most debris out, so gutters stay flowing during storms and need cleaning far less often.

They aren't maintenance-free. Every guard needs an occasional rinse or brush-off. We'll match the guard type — micro-mesh, screen, or hood-style — to the debris your trees actually drop.

How often should gutters be cleaned in Tampa Bay?

Many homes need cleaning at least twice a year; homes under heavy tree cover may need it more often. Our trees shed year-round, and oak pollen tassels in spring can clog a gutter fast.

If you do one cleaning a year, schedule it before hurricane season starts on June 1.

How do I get my gutters ready for hurricane season?
  • Clean troughs and flush downspouts so they drain at full capacity.
  • Look for sagging runs or gaps between the gutter and fascia.
  • Replace loose spikes with screw-fastened, hurricane-rated hidden hangers.
  • Repair soft or rotted fascia — hangers can't hold in bad wood.
  • Make sure downspouts discharge well away from the foundation.

We offer pre-season inspections, cleanings, and hanger upgrades. Request one.

Can overflowing gutters damage my fascia?

Yes, and it's one of the most common problems we see. Overflow and leaky seams soak the fascia board behind the gutter. In Florida humidity, that wood rots quickly, then hangers loosen and gutters sag — which causes more overflow.

Signs include peeling paint, dark staining, soft spots, or gutters pulling away. We replace damaged fascia before installing new gutters and can wrap it in color-matched aluminum. Fascia & soffit services.

Should I repair or replace my gutters?

Isolated problems — a leaking corner, a few loose hangers, one sagging run, or a clogged downspout — are usually worth repairing.

Replacement tends to be the better value when leaks show up at joints all along the system, gutters are undersized and overflow every heavy rain, or fascia rot runs along much of the roofline. We'll tell you which category you're in and price both options when it's a close call.
